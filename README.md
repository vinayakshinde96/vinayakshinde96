### 🎓 Placement CRM — Project Deep Dive

A complete **placement management platform** connecting Students, the Placement Cell (Admin), and Companies — built and documented in phases: requirement gathering, functional flow, database design, UML modeling, backend logic, frontend flow, admin operations, and deployment/testing.

<details>
<summary><b>📌 Click to expand full module breakdown</b></summary>

<br>

**👨‍🎓 Student Module**
- Registration & Login
- Profile completion (Name, Email, Phone, Address, Skills, Qualification, Experience, Domain, Hobbies, Certifications, Projects, LinkedIn, GitHub, Photo, Portfolio)
- Resume upload
- Skills & eligibility check
- Apply for job / opt out from placement
- View real-time interview status

**🛠️ Admin / Placement Cell Module**
- Approve student registrations
- Add companies & job requirements
- Filter students (domain, passout year, location, package, etc.)
- Track applications & update student status
- Maintain interview records, company visit data & hiring history

**🏢 Company Module**
- Company registration & profile
- Post multiple job requirements
- Define eligibility criteria
- Upload visit photos
- Update hiring status, add comments/notes
- Repost future requirements

**📊 Dashboard Metrics**
- Total companies
- Total job openings
- Total students
- Students currently in process
- Placed students
- Today's job openings
- Today's registrations

**🔄 High-Level Flow**

```
Student Registers
      ↓
Admin Approves Student
      ↓
Company Adds Job Requirement
      ↓
Eligible Students Notified
      ↓
Student Applies / Opts Out
      ↓
Admin Tracks Applications
      ↓
Company Shortlists Students
      ↓
Interview Conducted
      ↓
Status Updated: Selected / Rejected
      ↓
Placement History Stored
```
